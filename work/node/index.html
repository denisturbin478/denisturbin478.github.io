Behind the designDesign decisions, experience flow & numbers
The organising idea

Keep the graph central. Make settings contextual.

I designed the canvas, searchable block library and node-specific panels as one coordinated experience. Colored connections distinguish relationships; contextual settings concentrate the detail around the selected node. Zoom, panning and a minimap provide different ways to stay oriented.

Design coverage

Four work areas, one continuous workflow.

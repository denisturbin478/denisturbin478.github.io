The organising idea

Let the learner choose a relevant starting point.

I started with onboarding and its transition into the course experience. Beginner and experienced-stylist routes give the structure a clear starting choice. Modular lessons, progress layouts and rewards screens continue that journey, with imagery and a pink-led visual direction supporting the beauty-education context.

Design coverage

A learning journey with reasons to return.

Coiffure Master is a hair-extension learning app. Its three core functions are onboarding, structured lessons and loyalty rewards. The reward interface accounts for completed lessons, streaks and referrals.

The outcome

A Framer website featured on DesignRush, built around the product story, founder and waitlist. The brand is listed in Avant Now’s accelerator roster and featured by European Women in VC × Evertrace.

Behind the designDesign decisions, experience flow & numbers
The organising idea

Make the page feel like a brand, then give it a destination.

The page moves between the Sunfreaks story, its vision, the product and the founder. The visual direction connects those chapters, while the recurring waitlist action gives the launch website a clear next step throughout the experience.

Business recognition

Product development

A product story with substance behind it.

The brand reports 75 early testers and more than 25 formula prototypes, with five planned Founder Edition shades. The website brings that development story, the founder and waitlist into one public destination.

TRBN One

A cinematic Framer portfolio template for independent creatives. An image- and video-ready opening leads into selected work, services, process and contact, with editable content throughout.

Scope
Framer template
Tools
Framer

Challenge

Turn a personal visual direction into a portfolio other creatives can adapt to their own work.

Solution

Built a one-page structure with desktop, tablet and phone layouts, keeping typography, colours, media, copy and links editable.

Template features

  • One-page portfolio structure
  • Desktop, tablet and phone layouts
  • Image- and video-ready hero
  • Work, services and process sections
  • About, pricing and contact sections
  • Editable type, colours, media and links

The outcome

A commercial portfolio template released on Framer Marketplace on 25 August 2026, with editable content and responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

Behind the designDesign decisions, experience flow & numbers
The organising idea

Build the story around the work.

The introduction establishes a point of view, selected projects make it tangible, and the service and process sections explain what collaboration could look like. Typography, colours, media, text and links remain editable, so the owner can change the presentation without starting from an empty canvas.

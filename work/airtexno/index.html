The organising idea

Put reassurance beside the action.

I organised the service catalogue around the repair customer’s needs and placed trust information near contact and request actions. The result is a design where the route to an enquiry follows the information someone needs before making it.

Experience flow

Answer the questions before the request

The design follows the practical decisions of someone looking for appliance repair.

  2. 02Understand the business
  3. 03Review practical answers

Client context

Trust at the point of a repair request.

AirTexno’s homepage displays a 5.0 rating and 248 reviews. The current homepage places those review signals beside service information and repair-request actions, giving visitors a way to assess the service before contacting the team.

Business scale

A repair business with thousands of completed jobs.

AirTexno’s Thumbtack profile states more than 3,700 successful repairs since 2016. The indexed profile also lists Top Pro status. The website design connects that established service reputation to appliance information and repair-request actions.

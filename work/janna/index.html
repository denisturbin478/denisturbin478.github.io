The organising idea

Let familiarity lead to contact.

I organised the experience around the teacher’s approach, lesson format, pricing and student feedback. WhatsApp actions connect those decisions to a familiar conversation channel, keeping contact close to the information that helps someone choose.

Teaching community

An established audience, with a direct home for lessons.

Janna’s teaching audience is around 10K followers on Instagram and 5K on Facebook, as reported by the project owner in October 2026. The website brings lesson formats, pricing, teaching approach and student feedback into one destination, with a direct path to start a conversation.

Teaching record

844 lessons. A 5/5 student rating.

Her Preply profile records 844 lessons and a 5/5 overall rating from 22 public student reviews. Janna also holds Professional Tutor and Super Tutor status. The website connects this established teaching practice to her own brand and inquiry journey.

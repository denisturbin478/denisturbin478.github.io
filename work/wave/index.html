Wave Checkup

A mobile interface exploration for a photo-led wellness concept. Account access, processing, results, subscriptions and audio content sit within a dark visual system with neon-green accents.

Challenge

Give a dense results experience a readable sequence from overview to individual indicators and related content.

Solution

Behind the designDesign decisions, experience flow & numbers
The organising idea

Give every stage a clear job.

Stitch AI helped explore interface variations from the product requirements. I reviewed those concepts for flow, hierarchy and consistency, then refined the selected direction in Figma. Dark surfaces and neon green establish the visual identity; the screen structure organises the sequence behind it.

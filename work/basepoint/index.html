Keeping the website maintainable

The handover covered text editing, publishing and checking desktop, tablet and mobile layouts after changes, alongside domain connection, analytics and a style guide.

Planning for ongoing maintenance

The initial hosting choice was Framer’s Basic plan. At handover, we discussed how traffic and bandwidth affect ongoing maintenance, including external video hosting as an option for keeping media manageable.

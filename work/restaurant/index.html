The organising idea

Make the call workspace a point of focus.

The call interface organises four kinds of information around the conversation: client context, call controls, the script and follow-up activity. A common component system connects that focused workspace to the wider CRM, using dark surfaces and purple accents to maintain a recognisable product language.

Design coverage

From the first lead to an active client.

The design connects four modules: the sales dashboard, pipeline, client profiles and POS interface. It maps the client journey through lead, demo, onboarding and active-client stages, with shared components keeping the workspaces consistent.

CRM & POS scope
Public product ecosystem

Business tools connected to customer-facing products.

The SABSUS developer catalogue includes public food-ordering apps such as Crisp’n’Fluff and Capl. That wider product ecosystem gives context to this CRM and POS interface design, connecting client management, sales, onboarding and point-of-sale workflows.

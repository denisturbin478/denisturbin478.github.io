Build a visual language around the benefits.

I paired benefit-led content with abstract 3D imagery, AI-generated visuals and interactive effects. The page structure gives each visual moment context, turning an unusual product constraint into a distinctive way to introduce the CRM.

Business context

A platform spanning 71 modules and 56 industry scenarios.

SABSUS presents a broad operating platform for local businesses and networks. Its official site lists 71 modules and 56 industry scenarios, while the company’s app is publicly distributed through the App Store and Google Play. The 2025 website design introduced the platform through abstract 3D imagery and an interactive visual presentation.

SABSUS platformApp StoreGoogle Play
Visual direction

A clear introduction to a complex product.

The original website brief called for a presentation without product screenshots. Abstract 3D imagery and interactive visuals communicate white-label branding, automation, integrations and permissions.

How we work

Scope, design & handover

  1. 01

    Define the project

    We’ll discuss the audience, the task, and what needs to change. Scope, deliverables, timing, and quote are agreed before work starts.

  2. 02

    Shape the design

    We’ll work through structure, visual direction, and key pages or screens, with feedback at agreed stages.

  3. 03

    Build & hand over

    Website work can include Framer development, responsive checks, and editing guidance. Product design ends with the deliverables your team needs next.

Questions.

Scope, timing and working together.

Can you handle design and development?

Yes. Website projects can include design and Framer development. Product work can be scoped for design and handoff to your development team.

What if I already have designs?

We can focus on the Framer build, responsive behaviour, and the CMS your team needs.

What should I prepare?

A short description is enough to begin. Your current site or product, references, target date, and existing content help define the scope.

Can we redesign an existing website or product?

Yes. Share the current version and explain what is difficult for visitors or users. We’ll agree on what needs to change.

Can my team update the website?

For Framer projects, we can plan editable content and CMS collections around what your team needs to maintain.

Do you also create copy and brand identity?

I can help structure content and work with your materials. Additional copy or identity work is discussed and scoped separately.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, and feedback. Share your target date, and I’ll confirm a schedule after reviewing the brief.

How do you use AI?

AI supports research, exploration, and prototypes. I set the direction, refine the output, and review the final work.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

Describe what you’re trying to achieve and where things stand today. We can use that as the starting point for the scope.

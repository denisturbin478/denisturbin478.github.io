How we work

Scope, live preview & launch

  1. 01

    Define the project

    We’ll discuss the audience, the task, and what needs to change. Scope, deliverables, timing, and quote are agreed before work starts.

  2. 02

    Iterate on a live preview

    You see a working site within days, not a stack of mockups. Notes go in the same day, so the direction settles quickly. Product work moves screen by screen, with feedback at agreed stages.

  3. 03

    Launch & hand over

    Websites go live with responsive checks, speed and SEO work, and editing guidance. Product design ends with the deliverables your team needs next.

Questions.

Scope, timing and working together.

Can you handle design and development?

Yes. Websites are designed and built by me, in Framer or Astro. Product work can be scoped for design and handoff to your development team.

What if I already have designs?

I can build them in Framer or Astro, with the responsive behaviour and the CMS your team needs.

Framer or Astro?

Framer if your team wants to edit pages on a visual canvas. Astro if speed, SEO and owning the code matter more. I’ll recommend one after reading your brief.

What should I prepare?

A short description is enough to begin. Your current site or product, references, target date, and existing content help define the scope.

Can we redesign an existing website or product?

Yes. Share the current version and explain what is difficult for visitors or users. We’ll agree on what needs to change.

Can my team update the website?

Yes. In Framer your team edits pages directly. In Astro, content lives in a CMS or in files, and AI tools can make the changes for you to review.

Do you also create copy and brand identity?

I can help structure content and work with your materials. Additional copy or identity work is discussed and scoped separately.

How long does a project take?

A landing page or focused website goes live in about a week once content and direction are agreed. Larger sites and product work take longer. Share your target date, and I’ll confirm a schedule after reviewing the brief.

How do you use AI?

AI speeds up research, prototypes and code, which is how a site can go live in a week. I set the direction, refine the output, and review the final work.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

Describe what you’re trying to achieve and where things stand today. We can use that as the starting point for the scope.

Responsibility & purpose

I worked on the website’s design and Framer development. The goal is to help prospective students understand Janna’s teaching approach, explore the lesson format and make contact.

How the current site works

The current site brings together her background, teaching approach, lesson information, pricing and student feedback. Booking and contact links open WhatsApp, where a prospective student can discuss lessons directly.

The inquiry journey

Visitors can review the teaching approach, lesson format and pricing before making contact. The booking and contact actions lead into a WhatsApp conversation with Janna, where the lesson details can be discussed.

In her September 2025 Contra review, Janna recommends my design and Framer development work following our collaboration on her school’s website.

Creative direction, supported by AI.

I work confidently with AI tools to explore ideas, build prototypes and prepare materials more efficiently. I choose where these tools add value and stay responsible for the result.

  1. 1

    Set the direction

    I define the problem, audience and creative approach. The brief guides the tools and the decisions.

  2. 2

    Explore and prepare

    AI helps me organise research, explore variations and prepare draft materials for review.

  3. 3

    Build with AI

    I use AI coding tools to develop working prototypes, refine interactions and reduce repetitive implementation work.

  4. 4

    Select and refine

    I decide what works, revise the output and check the details. Creative judgement and final quality remain my responsibility.

The concept, visual direction and creative decisions are mine. I select, edit and check AI-assisted output against the brief before it becomes part of the final work.
